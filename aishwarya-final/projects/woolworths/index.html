Objective

To analyse Woolworths retail transaction data and identify customer purchasing behaviour, product performance, and cross-selling opportunities using Microsoft Excel — transforming raw sales data into actionable business intelligence.

Key Performance Indicators

$117,124 Total Revenue
20,720 Total Quantity Sold
Diapers Top Revenue Product
Wet Wipes Top Selling Product

Approach

  1. Cleaned and structured raw Woolworths retail transaction data for analysis.
  2. Built Pivot Tables to break down revenue and quantity sold by product category.
  3. Created Pivot Charts for visual comparison of product performance across key metrics.
  4. Performed correlation analysis to identify complementary purchasing patterns across product pairs.
  5. Designed an Excel dashboard with conditional formatting and data visualisations for stakeholder reporting.

Tools Used

Microsoft Excel — Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Correlation Analysis, Conditional Formatting, Dashboard Design.

Key Insights

  • Wet Wipes recorded the highest purchase volume (4,045 units), indicating strong recurring customer demand.
  • Diapers generated the highest overall revenue ($23,625) despite lower sales volume, pointing to high unit value.
  • Bread and Milk consistently performed well, reflecting strong everyday customer demand.
  • Salsa had the lowest sales volume and revenue, suggesting a need for visibility and promotional support.
  • Correlation analysis revealed clear complementary buying behaviour suitable for cross-selling strategies.

Business Recommendations

  • Bundle Pasta and Pasta Sauce to increase average basket value.
  • Position Nachos and Salsa together on shelves to encourage complementary purchases.
  • Promote Diapers and Wet Wipes through family-focused marketing campaigns.
  • Introduce Milk and Bread combo offers to drive repeat purchases.
  • Improve Salsa visibility through discounts and bundle promotions.

Outcome

Transformed raw retail transaction data into a fully interactive Excel dashboard delivering clear revenue analysis, quantity trends, and market basket insights — providing actionable recommendations to improve cross-selling strategies, merchandising decisions, and customer purchasing experience.
