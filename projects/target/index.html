Business Objective

The objective of this project was to analyse Target retail transaction data and compare performance across Store and Pickup/Delivery fulfilment channels.

The dashboard was developed to identify sales trends, high-performing categories, valuable customers, regional contribution, and manager performance, enabling stakeholders to make informed decisions relating to inventory, customer engagement, channel strategy, and resource allocation.

Key Insights

  • Store and Pickup/Delivery channels displayed different sales patterns, highlighting the importance of evaluating each channel independently.
  • Furniture emerged as the top-performing product category, contributing strongly to overall sales revenue.
  • New South Wales generated the highest state-level sales contribution, indicating stronger customer demand in this region.
  • A small number of buyers contributed a significant proportion of total revenue, identifying an opportunity to focus on high-value customer relationships.
  • Sales performance varied across managers and locations, creating opportunities for performance benchmarking and targeted operational support.
  • Monthly and category-level analysis revealed changes in sales performance over time, helping identify stronger and weaker periods.
  • Interactive slicers allowed users to analyse performance dynamically across fulfilment channel, category, buyer, manager, and state.

Business Recommendations

  • Monitor Store and Pickup/Delivery performance separately and develop channel-specific sales and fulfilment strategies.
  • Prioritise inventory availability and promotional activity for consistently high-performing categories such as Furniture.
  • Allocate stock, staffing, and marketing resources based on state-level demand and regional sales contribution.
  • Develop loyalty and retention initiatives for high-value buyers who contribute a large share of total revenue.
  • Use manager-level performance metrics to identify best practices, benchmark results, and provide targeted operational support.
  • Review lower-performing categories and regions to identify opportunities for pricing, promotional, and inventory improvements.
  • Use the interactive dashboard as a recurring reporting tool to support faster and more consistent sales decisions.

Outcome

The project transformed raw Target retail transaction data into an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard that provided a consolidated view of sales performance across fulfilment channels, categories, customers, managers, and states.

The dashboard enabled users to explore business performance dynamically, identify major revenue contributors, compare Store and Pickup/Delivery results, and support data-driven decisions relating to inventory planning, customer engagement, regional strategy, and operational performance.

Approach

  1. Reviewed and cleaned the raw Target retail transaction dataset to improve consistency and prepare it for analysis.
  2. Structured fields relating to sales, fulfilment channel, category, buyer, manager, state, and transaction date.
  3. Created Pivot Tables to summarise sales performance by month, product category, state, customer, manager, and fulfilment channel.
  4. Built Pivot Charts to compare sales trends and highlight performance differences across key business dimensions.
  5. Developed KPI cards to present total revenue, transaction volume, top-performing category, and highest-contributing state.
  6. Added interactive slicers to allow users to filter the dashboard by channel, category, state, buyer, and manager.
  7. Applied conditional formatting and dashboard design principles to improve readability, consistency, and ease of use.
  8. Converted the results into business insights and recommendations for channel strategy, inventory planning, customer retention, and regional resource allocation.
